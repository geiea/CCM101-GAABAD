# Mission Reflection

Completing the Multi-Cloud Explorer mission helped me understand the differences and similarities between AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP). Among the three platforms, **Google Cloud Platform impressed me the most** because of its strong focus on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Kubernetes. I found its services useful for modern applications and technologies that are becoming important in the IT industry.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities because they all provide cloud-based computing, storage, networking, databases, security, and identity management services. They also allow organizations to scale their resources depending on their needs and provide services that can support different types of applications. Although their services are similar, each platform has different strengths and features that can make one provider more suitable than another.

Business requirements have an important role in selecting a cloud platform. Factors such as budget, existing technologies, scalability, security, performance, and the type of application can affect the decision. For example, Azure is a good choice for organizations that already use Microsoft technologies, while GCP can be suitable for AI and Machine Learning projects.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about selecting the most popular platform. I learned that the best choice should be based on the specific needs and goals of the organization. Comparing services and analyzing different business scenarios helped me develop better decision-making skills.

Lastly, my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio improved because I was able to organize my research, comparisons, recommendations, Linux investigation, and reflection into separate Markdown files. This mission also helped me practice technical documentation, Markdown formatting, cloud service comparison, and maintaining an organized project repository. Overall, the activity gave me a better understanding of how cloud platforms are evaluated in real-world situations.
